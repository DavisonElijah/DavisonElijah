## 🧪 Selected Research Projects

### Epidemic-Aware Evacuation Allocation
*Mixed-Integer Optimization · Statistical Risk Modeling · Gurobi*

Developed an optimization framework integrating fitted spatiotemporal epidemic models with capacity-constrained evacuation allocation.

- Formulated a mixed-integer allocation model with dynamically evaluated epidemic-risk constraints
- Implemented the approach in Python and Gurobi using lazy-constraint callbacks
- Evaluated the framework on synthetic experiments and a 30-region Hurricane Sally case study

**Research output:** Manuscript under review.

### Dynamic Mobility Network Segmentation
*Time-Series Analysis · Hierarchical Clustering · Spatiotemporal Modeling*

Developed a segmentation approach for identifying changes in origin–destination mobility networks and incorporating time-varying spatial relationships into infectious disease forecasting.

- Identified mobility regimes through hierarchical clustering
- Estimated time-varying coupling between geographic regions
- Compared forecasting performance with alternative mobility representations

**Publication:** [IISE Transactions on Healthcare Systems Engineering](https://doi.org/10.1080/24725579.2025.2569354)

### Statistical Inference of Contact Networks
*LASSO · Sparse Estimation · Network Inference*

Co-developed a regularized statistical framework for reconstructing latent human contact networks from epidemic observations.

- Applied sparse estimation to infer network structure from limited data
- Evaluated the approach through simulations and county-level epidemic data

**Publication:** [IISE Transactions on Healthcare Systems Engineering](https://doi.org/10.1080/24725579.2024.2401466)

### Spatiotemporal Modeling of Connected-Vehicle Data
*Generalized Additive Models · GPS Analytics · Demand Forecasting*

Analyzed large-scale connected-vehicle movement data to characterize evacuation behavior and model demand for non-congregate shelter during hurricane-pandemic conditions.

- Processed approximately one billion connected-vehicle GPS waypoints
- Developed spatiotemporal demand models using generalized additive models and geographic information
- Evaluated performance against simpler statistical baselines

**Publication:** [Applied Sciences](https://doi.org/10.3390/app15063185)
